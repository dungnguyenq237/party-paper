Charades: printable game and host guide

4–12 players · 15–25 minutes · two teams

A low-preparation team game where one person acts and teammates guess. These everyday scenes work without celebrity knowledge or inside jokes.

Make this game

How to play

  1. Cut out the cards and fold the prompt inward. Put them in a bowl away from the guessing team.
  2. Teams alternate actors. The actor draws one card and has 60 seconds to mime it without speech, writing or pointing to objects with the answer on them.
  3. Award one point for a correct guess. Allow one pass per turn; a passed card goes to the bottom of the bowl.
  4. Play the same number of turns for each team. If tied, let both teams share the win or play one extra turn each.

Set up the printable

One action or scene per line. The generator lays out six cut-out cards per sheet.

Choose “Make my printables”, inspect every preview sheet, then use “Print / Save PDF”. Player pages appear first and any answer keys follow. In your print dialog, choose only the player page range for guests.

Make it work for your group

For younger players, use simple actions such as brushing teeth. For players who prefer not to perform, offer a timekeeper or scorekeeper role. Never require physical contact.

Host notes

Give the actor space and a clear view of the timer. Preview prompts before printing so none rely on a private story or make a guest uncomfortable.
