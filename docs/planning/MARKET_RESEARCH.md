# Market research and differentiation

Desk research reviewed 26 November 2026. “Observed” refers to the cited product, publisher, storefront or community listing. Weaknesses below describe a mismatch with the proposed audience. They are hypotheses for playtests, not findings about competitors' quality. Sales, retention, Vietnamese search volume and addressable revenue are unknown.

[Publisher](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/new-angeles/products/new-angeles/) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/945360/Among_Us/) | Those games give groups a shared threat or an immediately readable traitor. ## Regional evidence and limits

Vietnam has a domestic boxed-game ecosystem as well as a direct mystery competitor. The US Steam and Jackbox listings show distribution for digital group games. European publishers and distributors show that paid negotiation games, council voting and expansions reach retail. These signals justify recruiting local playtest groups. They cannot quantify the paying audience for browser rooms. [Board Game VN](https://boardgame.vn/products/khu-rung-kim-cuong-lop-hoc-mat-ngu) · [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/945360/Among_Us/) · [CGE](https://www.czechgames.com/games/last-will)

Mainland China's scripted mystery culture has been documented by Xinhua. The source dates to 2021 and provides historical context. Japan's Uzu lists a mobile distribution model. South Korea's CJ ENM describes an entertainment format built around casting and solving cases. Television viewership is an indirect signal for interest in deduction. Stage Chinese, Japanese and Korean localization only after local partnerships and editorial/device testing. Taiwan's night-market title is separate evidence from the mainland research. [Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/2021-09/09/c_1127842041.htm) · [Uzu mobile listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.sally_inc.uzu) · [CJ ENM](https://www.cjenm.com/en/featured-contents/crime-scene-returns/)

Open-source boardgame.io demonstrates reusable turn and networking primitives. PlayingCards.io describes shared virtual tables. Room codes and synchronized boards are already available substitutes. The differentiator must be authored rules, frictionless hosting and thoughtful recovery. Prototype room contracts against two very different games before evaluating a framework dependency. [boardgame.io](https://github.com/boardgameio/boardgame.io) · [PlayingCards.io](https://playingcards.io/docs/faq)

## Research before investment

Recruit casual hosts, strategy clubs and venue MCs in both languages. Observe a full session with first-time hosts using the written instructions. Record time to first action, turns passed, disputes over interpretation and who asks to play again. Interview planners separately about rehearsal, price, cancellation and connectivity. Ask mystery groups to buy or reserve a second case, with price disclosed. Use permissioned pilot recruitment and report sample sizes, language, group size and recruitment source. Check local marks and title collisions before commissioning identities. No market-size estimate or product-market fit claim follows from a listing.

## Independent review correction (IR-H07)

Competitor descriptions establish that alternatives exist, not adoption, retention, revenue or quality. Friend Court's hypotheses are harmless remedies, paired evidence, bilingual play without a TV, equitable role rotation and optional typed/pass paths—not invention of browser debate. Compare with an actual browser substitute and Corporate Chaos at P0. For each subsequent title record browser/mobile/physical alternatives in target languages, observation date and first-hand play evidence where obtainable. No uncontested-market or first-in-Vietnam claim without stronger evidence. Recruitment and real group preference determine priorities.
