# Buyer, paid boundary and readiness

Initial revenue assumptions are hypotheses. Keep complete base games, accessibility, consent, reconnect and fair scoring available to all participants. Charge the host for content, customization or a booked service. Do not charge guests to join, ask for votes in exchange for ads or sell advantages.

| Title | Free / candidate paid offer | Relative potential and driver | Cost, risk and delivery complexity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Friend Court | Full base docket · curated occasion packs | Medium distribution, low price per group | Low compute, recurring localized comedy editing. Test pack interest after a rematch · aggressive upsell interrupts laughter. Low commerce complexity. |
| Corporate Chaos | Full base roles and mandates · facilitated debrief and scenario license | Medium–high if teams repeat | Low compute, high facilitator/support effort. No individual employee evaluation. Medium fulfillment. |
| Night Market | Complete 4-evening market · festival rules and visual stalls | Medium niche, repeat strategy play | Low compute, balance/editorial cost for each addition. Cosmetic or symmetric changes only. Medium. |
| Cold Case Vietnam | One complete case · one-off host unlock for a new authored case, whole room joins | High candidate for first digital purchase | Low runtime · writing, editing, native review and blind testing dominate. A spoiled case rarely resells to the same group. Medium entitlement complexity. |
| Inheritance War | Base will and full player count · standalone estate packs | Medium–low until replay observed | Low compute, high tuning and copy effort. Avoid social pressure on families. Medium. |
| Wedding Arena | Guided rehearsal/demo · one-event pass with optional custom theme, prep or staffed support | High potential contract value per planner booking | Bursty load and human event-day support, refunds and rehearsal. Distinct organizer/projector permissions. High commercial complexity. |
| Voice Telephone | Complete short private chain or text game · curated sound prompts | Low until consent and real-device completion are strong | Bandwidth, deletion, abuse reporting and support. Longer retention is never a paid perk. High operational complexity. |
| AI Battle Arena | Complete replay briefs · reviewed challenge packs · future prepaid live rounds | Uncertain, conditional on margins and repeated play | Replay has zero per-match inference, editorial corpus still costs money. Live modes have marginal cost and quota abuse risk. Medium for packs, high for live. |

**Illustrative break-even, not tested prices.** A bilingual case costing $450 to write/test and netting $4.50 per host after fees, refunds, tax reserve and variable delivery needs 100 paid unlocks before fixed overhead. A $50 event pass with $3 fees/refund reserve, $2 attributable hosting and 90 minutes support at $15/h yields $22.50 before acquisition and product development. A delayed event needing two extra support hours loses $7.50 on that sale. Interview local buyers using VND and international buyers in their own currency. Check invoices/tax, pricing display and refunds with the operator. Offer concierge fulfillment for a small contracted pilot before building automated checkout. Subscriptions need a demonstrated publishing cadence or repeat venues. Sponsors may fund a clearly disclosed pack subject to editorial independence and venue/privacy review.

## AdSense and paid launch gates

The existing print guides, working generator, About, privacy and contact are assets. Publisher metadata/ads.txt exist, slot ID is empty, siteApproved and enabled are false. Twelve canonical pages plus useful games do not establish approval. Verify Search Console coverage and actual owner dashboard decisions. Audit thin or duplicated locale pages, old claims of all-local behavior, privacy choices, working private contact (current contact is GitHub), operator identity, terms, cancellations and accessible navigation. Publish complete original guides, editorial credit and review dates appropriate to each game. Keep under-construction titles out of public catalog/sitemap. [Repository audit](REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md) · [Google eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724) · [Original content guidance](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/81904)

Limit any future ad slot to an editorial/public guide after useful content. Keep private rooms, active games, projector, results, print/PDF and paid event experiences free of ad scripts. Verify current consent requirements, implement certified CMP where required with withdrawal, reserved layout, no-fill/timeout handling, operator approval and publisher/slot configuration. Retain existing build guard until a separately reviewed integration. The creator must be able to disable all new requests without affecting games. [Existing ads runbook](../../ADS.md) · [Google publisher policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10502938)

Before automated sales, establish order/entitlement ID, deduplicated checkout callback, guest recovery receipt, refund and support workflow. Test that a replayed payment event cannot duplicate grants and a refund does not terminate an event in progress. Store bare minimum order records with operator-defined legally reviewed retention, separate from transient room identities. Record current bills, support hours, cost per full session and repeat buyers before extending monetization.

## Independent review: commercial gates (IR-H07, IR-M02, IR-M06–10)

Contribution = collected revenue minus refunds/tax reserve, payment fees, attributable hosting/egress, editorial amortization, acquisition and actual operator hours. Example $450 case/$4.50 net needs 100 unlocks; at $2.25 net needs 200. Neither is a forecast. Example $50 event with $5 fees/hosting and 3.5 support hours at $15/h loses $7.50 before acquisition. Quote rehearsal, event availability, travel and refund risk separately; require real buyer commitments and named support owner before checkout. Voice at 12 seats has 12 MiB accepted hard maximum plus partial uploads and playback; text-first, no paid retention. AI six briefs × 12 cells × two locales = 144 reviewed outputs if full matrices retained; zero inference is not zero editorial cost. No guaranteed SEO/approval/revenue. Verify actual dashboard, consent obligations, traffic and customer intent; no thin placeholders. Ads remain off. Do not launch paid functionality merely because the engine works.
