Party Paper field guide

Make bingo cards and word searches worth gathering for.

Create a custom bingo set

  1. Choose “Party bingo / 5 × 5” and give the game a title.
  2. Add at least 24 different items, one per line. Keep each item under 46 characters so it fits on the printed card.
  3. Choose 1–30 cards and press “Make my printables.” Every card has a unique arrangement and a free center.
  4. Use the preview arrows to inspect every card. Print only after you are happy with the text.

Choose a theme people understand

For a birthday, try moments such as “A group photo,” “Someone tells a childhood story” and “The birthday song.” For a team gathering, use harmless shared experiences such as “Someone mentions coffee” or “A pet appears on camera.” Avoid embarrassing or private details.

Explain the rules before you start: players mark an item when it happens, and five squares in a row, column or diagonal wins. The middle square is already free. You can choose a different pattern if everyone agrees.

Create a word search

  1. Switch to “Word search.” Add up to 20 words, one per line.
  2. Use English letters. Spaces and hyphens are removed, so “BIRTHDAY CAKE” becomes “BIRTHDAYCAKE.”
  3. Choose a 12 × 12, 14 × 14 or 18 × 18 grid. Use a larger grid for longer words or more entries.
  4. Generate the puzzle. The second preview sheet is the answer key, with start and end row/column positions counted from 1.

Words can run across, down or diagonally. If the words do not fit, choose a larger grid or shorten the list and generate again. Keep the answer sheet separate from player copies.

Print or save as PDF

Click “Print / Save PDF” to open your browser's print dialog. Choose a printer or the available PDF destination. The layout targets A4; inspect the print preview and adjust scaling for other paper sizes. Browser menus and printer behavior vary by device.

Application controls and advertising slots are excluded from the printable sheets. If your browser adds its own URL or date headers, turn off “Headers and footers” in the print settings. Use the page-range control to print only the puzzle when you want to keep its answer key private.

Changing your mind?

Editing words or settings marks the previous preview as outdated. Generate again before printing. Switching game modes preserves each mode's draft during the current session; reloading the page clears your work.
