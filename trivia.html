Custom trivia: printable game and host guide

2–20 players · 15–20 minutes · individuals or teams

A printable quiz with a separate host answer key. Start with the supplied everyday-science round or replace it with facts your guests can reasonably know.

Make this game

How to play

  1. Enter each question and answer separated by a vertical bar. Check factual answers before printing; the tool formats your text and does not fact-check it.
  2. Give players the question sheets only. Keep the final answer-key pages with the host.
  3. Read each question aloud and give everyone the same thinking time. Ask teams to write one answer before moving on.
  4. Award one point per correct answer. Agree on spelling tolerance and accepted equivalents in advance. For a tie, use a new question with equal chances for both teams or share the result.

Ready-to-use pack: Everyday science quiz

These prompts are included in the generator. Edit any item to suit your guests before printing.

  • Which planet is known as the Red Planet? | Mars
  • What gas do plants absorb during photosynthesis? | Carbon dioxide
  • How many sides does a hexagon have? | Six
  • What is the chemical symbol for gold? | Au
  • What force keeps planets in orbit around the Sun? | Gravity
  • What part of a plant absorbs water from soil? | Roots
  • At sea level, at what Celsius temperature does pure water freeze? | 0 degrees Celsius
  • What is the largest organ of the human body? | Skin

Set up the printable

One question | answer pair per line. Player sheets omit answers; separate answer sheets follow at the end.

Choose “Make my printables”, inspect every preview sheet, then use “Print / Save PDF”. Player pages appear first and any answer keys follow. In your print dialog, choose only the player page range for guests.

Make it work for your group

Read questions aloud as well as printing them. For mixed ages, add a balanced mix of topics and let younger guests join a team.

Host notes

Do not give one team an advantage with questions based on a private memory. If an answer is disputed and cannot be verified, remove that question from every team’s score.

Your edits stay in this tab and are cleared on reload. Save a PDF before closing if you want to keep the result. See the printing guide for paper size and scaling.
