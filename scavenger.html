Scavenger hunt: printable game and host guide

2–12 players · 10–15 minutes · solo or pairs

A relaxed observation hunt for a shared indoor space. Players identify objects rather than race to collect them, so furniture and personal belongings can stay where they are.

Make this game

How to play

  1. Before play, define the allowed rooms and mark private or off-limits areas. Read every clue and remove anything that does not fit your venue.
  2. Give each player or pair a checklist. They write an object or location beside each clue rather than taking the object.
  3. Set a 10-minute timer. Award one point for each answer that the host can verify; multiple answers to a clue may be valid.
  4. Invite each pair to share one surprising find. For a tie, ask tied teams to explain their most creative valid answer and let the group choose, or share the win.

Ready-to-use pack: Look closer: indoor hunt

These prompts are included in the generator. Edit any item to suit your guests before printing.

  • Something round
  • Something made of wood
  • An object with three colors
  • Something that makes a quiet sound
  • A pattern with stripes
  • Something you can recycle
  • An object older than a year
  • Something soft
  • An item with a handle
  • Something that reminds you of a trip
  • A tiny detail you never noticed
  • Something shaped like a letter

Set up the printable

One clue per line. Each printable has checkboxes and space for written answers.

Choose “Make my printables”, inspect every preview sheet, then use “Print / Save PDF”. Player pages appear first and any answer keys follow. In your print dialog, choose only the player page range for guests.

Make it work for your group

Use a single accessible room and remove the timer if needed. For children, an adult should supervise and agree which objects may be touched.

Host notes

Do not include climbing, food tasting, street crossings, opening cupboards or photographing people without permission. A seated observation hunt can be just as interesting.
