Choose the right energy

For guests arriving at different times, set out a word search or word scramble. Use conversation cards for a seated table, charades or drawing for team play, and the observation hunt when people want to explore the room. Trivia needs a host who keeps the answer sheet separate; bingo works throughout a gathering.

Prepare before guests arrive

Preview the entire set, check names and answers, print one test page, and prepare pencils. Decide whether the game is cooperative or scored. Explain the stopping point, tie rule and option to pass before the first round. No game requires buying a prize.
