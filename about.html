Good games start with your own words.

Party Paper is a free tool for making printable party bingo cards and word search puzzles. Turn names, shared memories or a theme into something your guests can play together.

Two ways to play

  • Create up to 30 unique 5 × 5 bingo cards with a free center.
  • Make a word search from up to 20 English-letter words, with a separate answer key.
  • Preview every sheet, then print or save a PDF through your browser.

Made for simple gatherings

There is no signup and your words are processed on your device. The tool does not provide ticket sales, wagering or prize administration. You choose the theme and rules for your gathering.
